Before the bridge, the river near Huataraque could not be crossed for about 150 days of
the year, which cut the community's 100 residents off from their primary and secondary
schools, their farmland, and the nearest hospital. In the three years before construction,
one person died trying to cross it on foot.

Our chapter designed the bridge with teams from McGill University and the University of
Illinois, and three of our members (Sage, Emerson, and Sofia) traveled to Bolivia to build
it with the community and local masons. The build team finished the 56-meter span by the
end of August 2024, and the crossing is now open all year.
