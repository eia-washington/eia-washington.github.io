The river at Tomata was impassable for about 150 days a year, and on the far side were the
community's schools, much of its farmland, and the places where it holds cultural events.

We partnered with Oregon State University on the design. In August 2025 our travelers
(Adya, Nathan, and Gabe) worked alongside the people of Tomata to finish the 66-meter
bridge, which now gives more than 275 people a safe crossing every day of the year.
