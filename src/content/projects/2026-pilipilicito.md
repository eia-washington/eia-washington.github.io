Pilipilicito sits in a lush part of Bolivia's Chuquisaca region, which is a very different
environment from the dry highland sites of our first two bridges. For about 150 days a
year the river kept the community's 100 residents from reaching preschool, primary and
secondary school, their farmland, transportation, the Thiyumayu Health Center, and social
and cultural events. In the three years before the project, one person died crossing it
on foot.

This was the longest bridge our chapter has worked on, and we designed it with teams from
Oregon State University, the University of Illinois, and Virginia Tech. The project was
funded by Engineers in Action, the municipality of Padilla, and the community itself,
with each university chapter raising its share.
